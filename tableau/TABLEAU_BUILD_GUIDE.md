
# Tableau Build Guide

## Dashboard 1 — KYC/EDD Executive Dashboard
Use a white background, thin light-gray borders, compact spacing, and the same visual standard as Project 1.

### KPI row
- Total Customers
- High / Critical Risk
- EDD Required
- Open KYC Cases
- KYC Complete
- PEP Customers

### Main visualizations
1. **Customer Risk Mix — Donut**
   - Low, Moderate, High, Critical
   - Percentages inside donut
   - Legend to the right

2. **KYC Completeness — Side-by-Side Bar**
   - Individual vs Business
   - Average KYC completeness %

3. **Top EDD Drivers — Horizontal Bar**
   - Sanctions
   - PEP
   - Adverse Media
   - High-Risk Geography
   - Source of Wealth
   - KYC Documentation

4. **Review Case Aging — Column**
   - 0–30
   - 31–60
   - 61–90
   - 90+

5. **Top High-Risk Countries — Horizontal Bar**
   - Show top five full country names

6. **KYC Case Status — Column**
   - Open
   - In Review
   - Awaiting Customer
   - Escalated
   - Closed

## Formatting
- Use K/M notation for large values.
- Keep labels fully visible.
- No stacked bars unless the metric is genuinely part-to-whole.
- Use full country names.
- Keep titles short.
- Avoid overlapping legends and labels.
- Use tiled containers so dashboard objects remain close and aligned.


## Final Teal Visual Standard
- Use teal shades for all charts and KPI accents.
- Do not use stacked bar charts.
- Keep the customer-risk donut smaller and reserve clear space on the right for its legend.
- Keep all labels and values fully visible.
- Use short titles.
- Maintain white dashboard background, light borders, and compact tiled spacing.


## Final Mixed-Color Standard
Keep the existing layout and chart types unchanged. Use a professional mixed palette across categories
(blue, green, amber, red, purple, teal). Do not use stacked bars. Preserve white backgrounds,
light borders, clear labels, and the smaller donut with legend space on the right.
